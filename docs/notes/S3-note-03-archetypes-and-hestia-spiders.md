# Archetype names and Hestia Spiders lists (Peter's notes)

Written by Peter Balasis on 2026-11-23 (version 2: clarified the deck descriptions and dropped a test side deck). This is author knowledge from a competitive player, not an official source. Unofficial Elestrals fan content: card names and counts only, no card text or art.

## Archetype names used in the November 21, 2026 Premier

- Hermes Toolbox: a Hermes deck that uses the core Hermes cards to adapt to each matchup. The November 21 winning list adds Galaxea to answer Apheros, which is common in the current meta.
- Hephestia: a deck that combines the Hephaestus and Hestia cores.
- Hades Trifernal/Sloth: a Hades deck named for its two bosses, Trifernal (Champion of Hades) and Neandersloth (Champion of Khione).
- Dio Duggy: pairs Dionysus with the Duggy and Duggabyss line.
- Dem Dio: runs several copies of both Demeter and Dionysus, but leans on Demeter.
- Dem Dio Hades: a Dem Dio list that adds Hades and Neandersloth.
- Lunar Aggro: a Lunar deck with no bosses and a lot of removal.

## What a Hestia Spiders deck is

Hestia Spiders is a Hestia deck that mixes in Wind cards and revolves around the new Kitemite and Parachne (both Champion of Hestia). It aims to swarm the board and deal as much damage as possible by combining Hestia's Hearth with Parachne's Battle Phase effect.

## What a Spider Bears deck is

Spider Bears combines Hestia Spiders with the swarming of Hestia Bears and the destruction effect of Majursa (Champion of Hestia). Some builds add any mix of Ursicle, Hibearnation and Ursa Den for extra pressure, a package players call "Bear Bomb". Peter has won local events with Spider Bears over the past few weeks. The Hestia Spiders Update list below cuts most of the bears for more spiders, including Arachne's Lair, and is what Peter plans to test in the week of 2026-11-23.
